# 📪 Campaign Management

TROPHÉE Unity SDK: Campaign Management

Once you've completed the verification process, you can manage and set up campaigns in your game using the **Campaign Editor Window**.

<details>

<summary><strong>Step 1: Access the Campaign Editor Window</strong></summary>

* After successful verification, navigate to the `Trophée` menu.
* Click on `CampaignEditorWindow`.
  * This will open the **Campaign Editor Window**, where you can select and customize your campaigns.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Step 2: Configure Campaign Settings</strong></summary>

* **Placement Type**:
  * Select how you want to display the campaign (e.g., **2D Placement**, **3D Placement**).
* **Orientation Mode**:
  * Choose between **Landscape Mode** or **Portrait Mode** depending on your game's layout.
* **Player Tag**:
  * Specify the player tag to target specific game entities for campaign interaction.
* **Additional Features**:
  * Enable/Disable animations and sound for the campaign by checking the respective boxes.
* **Gratification Text**:
  * Enter a custom text to be displayed as part of the campaign’s gratification message.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Step 4: Instantiate the Campaign</strong></summary>

After configuring all the settings, click the `Instantiate` button to activate the campaign within your game.

* This will place the selected campaign with the desired settings into the game environment.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Step 5: Refresh Campaign List</strong></summary>

To check for new or updated campaigns, click the `Refresh` button at the bottom left of the window.

</details>

# 🧩 Retro TV Placement Guide

**Trophée Campaign Editor Integration**

The **Retro TV Placement** is a dynamic in-game video ad unit designed to display **non-intrusive, immersive brand videos** directly inside your gameplay environment. It can be positioned freely within your scene, blending naturally into the game world.

***

#### ⚙️ Step-by-Step Setup Guide

**Step 1: Open Campaign Editor Window**

1. In Unity, navigate to\
   **Trophée → Campaign Editor Window**
2. The Campaign Editor Window will open, displaying the campaign configuration interface.

***

**Step 2: Select Placement Type**

1. Under **Placement Type**, open the dropdown menu.
2. Select **Retro TV Placement** from the list.

🖼️ _Preview:_\
When you select “Retro TV Placement,” a **Retro TV prefab** will automatically appear in your Scene view.

***

**Step 3: Position the Placement**

1. Locate the newly spawned **RetroTVScreen** object under the **Canvas** hierarchy in your **Scene**.
   * Path: `Canvas > RetroTVScreen`
2. Move, rotate, or scale the TV object to fit your environment — for example:
   * Beside pathways
   * Near checkpoints
   * Inside interactive zones

💡 _Tip:_ Place it at natural viewing angles to ensure seamless visual integration.

***

**Step 4: Instantiate in Scene**

Once configured, click **Instantiate** to finalize the placement.\
Your Retro TV will now dynamically fetch and display **high-quality brand video ads** during gameplay.

***

#### 🎮 Example Use Cases

* Display in **idle zones** or **level transitions** for natural ad visibility.
* Show ads **post-gameplay**, such as at “Game Over” or “Victory” screens.
* Integrate into **in-world props** for an immersive, story-aligned experience.

***

#### 🧠 Developer Notes

* The placement is **non-intrusive** and **consent-first** — it never interrupts gameplay.
* Ads are **optimized for in-game rendering**, ensuring **high-quality visuals** and **smooth playback**.
* This format yields **higher fill rates and CPMs** due to premium brand inventory.<br>

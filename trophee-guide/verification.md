# 📎 Verification

This guide outlines the steps for verifying your credentials using the TROPHÉE Unity SDK.

<details>

<summary><strong>Step 1: Access the Verification Window</strong></summary>

* In Unity, navigate to the top menu bar.
* Click on the `Trophée` tab.
* From the dropdown menu, select `Verification`.
  * This will open the **Verification Window**.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Step 2: Verify Yourself</strong></summary>

* _"Please Verify Yourself"_
* Enter your **Publisher ID** and **Game ID** in the respective fields on the right panel under the **Security Token Manager** script.
  * **Publisher ID**: Your unique identifier provided by TROPHÉE.
  * **Game ID**: The unique ID of the game you are integrating with TROPHÉE.
* After entering the IDs, click the `Verify Now` button in the Verification window.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png>)

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Step 3: Successful Verification</strong></summary>

* Upon successful verification, you will see a message indicating that the **Campaign Editor Window** will become active.
* The verification status will be updated in the window, and additional features, such as campaign access and campaign management, will be unlocked.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Step 4: Campaign Management</strong></summary>

* Once verified, you can navigate to the `Trophée` menu again.
* The `CampaignEditorWindow` will now be accessible, allowing you to manage your game commerce campaigns.

\
![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (1).png>)

</details>

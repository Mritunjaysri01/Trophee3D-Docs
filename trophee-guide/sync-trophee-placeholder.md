# 👉 Sync Trophée Placeholder

In the **Campaign Editor Window**, you will find tools that help you manage ad placements within your game. These tools are designed to enhance your workflow by giving you full control over placement visibility and synchronization. The **Refresh Placements** button allows you to quickly discover and display all active placements from your current game scene inside the editor, while the **Sync Placements** button ensures these placements are connected with the Trophee server.

By combining both, you can seamlessly manage placements, keep your editor updated, and allow Trophee to monitor and optimize monetization opportunities in real time.

#### **Refresh Placements**

* **Function:** Scans your active game scene and identifies all available ad placements.
* **Purpose:** Updates the Campaign Editor UI to display every placement currently active in the scene.
*   **Usage Scenario:**

    * When you add, remove, or modify placements in your game scene and want to see the latest list in the UI tool.
    * Ensures that you are always working with the most accurate and up-to-date set of placements.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (34).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### **Sync Placements**

* **Function:** Syncs the placements from your game scene with the Trophee server.
* **Purpose:** Allows Trophee to monitor placement performance and optimize monetization opportunities for your game.
* **Usage Scenario:**
  * After creating or refreshing placements, you should **sync** them so that Trophee’s backend can dynamically select, deliver, and optimize campaigns.
  * Essential for enabling campaign tracking, performance insights, and real-time optimization.

***

⚡ **Workflow Recommendation:**

1. Click **Refresh Placements** after making changes in your Unity scene.
2. Once the placements are confirmed in the UI, click **Sync Placements** to push updates to Trophee’s server.

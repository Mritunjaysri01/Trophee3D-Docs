# ✨ Trophée Commerce Ecosystem

## In Game Product Placement  (3D/2D)

In Game Product Placement (3D/2D) is a powerful feature of our SDK that allows developers to seamlessly integrate branded products or advertisements into their games. This feature supports both 3D and 2D environments, providing flexibility for various game types.

Key aspects of In Game Product Placement include:

* Dynamic placement: Products can be inserted or updated in real-time, allowing for timely and relevant advertising.
* Customizable integration: Developers can control how products appear in their game world, ensuring they fit naturally within the game's aesthetic and gameplay.
* Performance optimization: Our SDK is designed to minimize the impact on game performance while rendering high-quality product placements.
* Analytics tracking: Measure user engagement with placed products to optimize advertising strategies.

This feature enables game developers to monetize their games effectively while maintaining a seamless user experience.

## In Game Play Gratification

In Game Play Gratification is a feature designed to enhance user engagement and retention by providing rewards or incentives during gameplay. This feature allows developers to implement various gratification mechanisms that encourage players to continue playing and achieve specific goals within the game.

Key aspects of In Game Play Gratification include:

* Reward systems: Implement customizable reward structures for completing tasks, reaching milestones, or achieving high scores.
* Progressive unlocks: Create a sense of progression by unlocking new content, features, or abilities as players advance.

This feature helps developers create more engaging and satisfying gaming experiences, potentially leading to increased player retention and monetization opportunities.

## Share with Friends

Share with Friends is a feature that enables players to share their brand deals and achievements with their social network. This feature enhances the social aspect of gaming while also promoting the brands involved in the game.

Key aspects of Share with Friends include:

* Social media integration: Allow players to easily share their brand deals on popular social media platforms.
* Customizable sharing options: Give players control over what they share and how they present their achievements.
* Referral rewards: Implement a system where players can earn bonuses for successfully referring friends to the game or specific brand deals.
* Viral marketing potential: Leverage player networks to increase the reach of in-game advertising and brand partnerships.
* Privacy controls: Ensure players have options to manage their privacy and control what information is shared.

This feature not only enhances player engagement but also provides additional value to brand partners by extending the reach of their in-game presence beyond the immediate player base.
